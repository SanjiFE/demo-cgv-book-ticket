Privacy policy

We collect the details you give us when you book, subscribe or contact us: your name, email, phone number and group size. We use them to run your booking, send confirmations and reminders, and reply to your messages.

Newsletter emails go only to people who subscribe, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We do not sell your details to anyone.

To see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, email contact@cgvexperience.vn.

Payment policy

Prices are per person, in Vietnamese dong, and include VAT. You can pay online by card, MoMo or VNPay, or pay at the counter when you arrive.

  • Online payments are taken when you confirm the booking.
  • Pay-at-counter bookings are held until 15 minutes before the slot starts.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days.

Cancellation policy

  • More than 24 hours before your slot: cancel for free from My bookings.
  • Within 24 hours: 50% of the total is kept.
  • No-shows: the full amount is kept.

If we have to cancel a session, for example for maintenance, we refund you in full and help you rebook.

Terms of use

By booking you agree to follow the instructions of our game masters and booth hosts, and to treat the rooms, props and equipment with care. Damage caused on purpose may be charged.

Players under 16 need an adult in the group. Food and drinks stay outside the rooms and booths.

This website is a demonstration. Bookings, payments and accounts are simulated and stored only in your browser.
