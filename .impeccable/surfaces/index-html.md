# Surface brief: CGV Experience site (all pages)

Scope: the whole static site (index.html + pages/*). Visitor mode: Persuade on home, experiences and service pages; Operate on booking, my bookings, notifications; Read on blog, post, policies. One world across all.

Audience/job: young Hanoi friend groups booking an escape room, photobooth or combo slot inside the CGV cinema, mostly on phones. Action: book a slot (4 steps). Proof: real photography from the source site, real prices/durations/party limits, live slot availability. Constraints: file://, vanilla, English, VND, no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every experience is a reel on the projector; the site counts you down to your slot. Refuses the category default of hero banner + three equal rounded service cards + generic stepper.

OWN-WORLD: Black film base (#0B0A0A / #15110F / #1E1916), leader gray #8E8A84, paper white #F2EDE6, CGV red #E52430 as edge print, grease pencil and the one primary action. Sprocket rails that hold real sequences (frames, steps, slots). Edge-print labels in Barlow Condensed caps with frame numbers; display in Big Shoulders Display 900; body Barlow. Small 4–6px frame corners. Depth only from stacked flat planes, no glow, no gradients. Raises: booking runs down one reel axis (Versailles); flat stacked planes, never glow (Łowicz); one grease-pencil circle marks the thing that matters per view (zine); every slot shows spots left, not just open/closed (six-pack).

STORY: Visitor sees the neon booth photo projected in a frame, understands the three experiences with price and time, sees the next open slots tonight, and books down the reel in four frames ending in a ticket with a countdown to their session.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width horizontal film strip: sprocket rails top and bottom with edge print; inside, the hero photo as the projected frame, headline "Your next great moment" at display scale left, countdown leader ring (3-2-1) top right that plays once, primary red "Book a slot" + secondary "See experiences" under the headline. Directly below, still in the first viewport on desktop, a "Tonight's reel" strip: three frames with service, price/person and next open time + spots left.

FORM: Countdown Leader (35mm film leader and projection booth), position 7 on the grounded list, seed key 714c1e8a.

Signature interaction: leader countdown ring with conic sweep on the hero and the confirmation ticket ("starts in"), and the reel advance when booking steps change (frame pulled into the gate, clip-path reveal). Grease-pencil ellipse draws on around the selected slot and total.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
